Before You Book

Common Questions.

Does my child need basketball experience?

No. Players can start at different experience levels. A beginner will work on foundations appropriate for their age, while more experienced players can be challenged at a higher level.

Do boys and girls train together?

No. Boys and girls train in separate groups. This gives players their own space to compete, learn and receive meaningful coaching attention.

What ages does LIT train?

LIT works with players from Ages 5–18.

Where does LIT train?

District 7, Ho Chi Minh City. Contact Coach for current group details.

How much is training?
Ages 5–11 350,000 VND / Session 60 minutes
Ages 12–18 425,000 VND / Session 90 minutes

Is LIT only about basketball IQ?

No. Strong basketball skills still matter. The goal is to become better at using those skills in the game — knowing when, where and why to use them.

How do I know which group is right?

Send Coach the player's age and current basketball experience.
