BEFORE YOU BOOK

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

Start with one session. We will help you understand where your athlete fits and what kind of challenge they need next.

Does my child need basketball experience?
No. Athletes join LIT Elite from different starting points. Some are trying basketball for the first time, while others already play and want more focused development.
How do you place athletes into the right group?
We consider age, experience, confidence, current skill level, and the challenge the athlete needs next. The trial session helps us understand the best fit.
Is LIT Elite too serious for a beginner?
No. The standard is high, but the challenge is adjusted to the athlete. Beginners are coached through movement, fundamentals, confidence, and simple game understanding.
Will experienced athletes be challenged?
Yes. More experienced athletes train with more pace, pressure, accountability, and decision-making. The goal is not just harder drills — it is better basketball understanding.
Who coaches the sessions?
LIT Elite is coach-led and built around Coach Zinger's coaching principles. Coach Zinger leads the program and remains actively involved in athlete development, supported by assistant coaches who follow the same standard.
What happens during a trial session?
Your athlete joins a real training session. We observe how they move, listen, respond, make decisions, and handle the challenge. After the session, we can recommend the right group or next step.
What does the package include?
Each package includes coach-led training sessions, a LIT Elite jersey, equipment use, and facility costs. One price. Nothing hidden.
What if I am not sure which program fits my child?
That is exactly what the trial is for. Start with one session, and we will help identify the right pathway based on your athlete's age, experience, and development needs.
