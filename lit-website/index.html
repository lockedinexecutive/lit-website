WHAT IS LIT ELITE BASKETBALL

WE BUILD ATHLETES FOR LIFE

Every athlete starts from a different place.

Some need stronger fundamentals. Some need better game understanding. Some need the confidence to play freely, make decisions, and trust themselves on the court.

LIT Elite Basketball exists to guide that journey.

Based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, LIT Elite is an international youth basketball academy for U7, U9, U11, and U15 athletes. Through purposeful, high-standard coaching, we help players improve their skills, correct their habits, understand the game, and build confidence through sport.

We believe confidence is trained. It grows through the right environment, the right feedback, and the right challenges. That is why our athletes are encouraged to try, make mistakes, learn, and keep growing.

Nothing is random. Every drill has a purpose. Every rep is designed to transfer to the game.

WE DON’T BELIEVE IN RANDOM REPS.
WE BELIEVE IN PURPOSEFUL DEVELOPMENT.

FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What ages does LIT Elite Basketball cater to?
We offer purposeful basketball training for U7, U9, U11, and U15 age groups, accommodating both beginners and experienced young athletes in Ho Chi Minh City.
Does my child need prior basketball experience to join?
Not at all! Our programs are designed to develop athletes from the ground up. Whether your child is picking up a basketball for the first time or looking to elevate their game, our small-group sessions provide the focused attention they need.
Who leads the training sessions?
All sessions are led by Coach Zinger, an experienced international coach with 15+ years of experience. He is committed to a hands-on, purposeful coaching approach for every single athlete.
What is included in the program fee?
The fee covers all sessions led by Coach Zinger, one custom LIT Elite Basketball jersey, and all equipment and facility costs. There are no hidden fees.
How do I sign up for a trial session?
You can easily book a trial session by clicking the "Book a Trial Session" button on this page or filling out our Google Form. A trial is just 380,000 VND and requires no long-term commitment.
