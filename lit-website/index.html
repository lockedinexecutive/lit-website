The real gap

Skills alone don't win games.

Your child practises dribbling, passing and shooting — but in a real game the defender moves, the space changes, and they hesitate. The talent is there; the decisions aren't yet.

Left unaddressed, that gap costs more than points. Talented kids get passed over at tryouts, lose confidence, and slowly stop enjoying the game they love.

That's exactly the gap LIT is built to close.

Before you book

Common questions, answered.

Does my child need basketball experience?
No. Players can start at different experience levels. A beginner will work on foundations appropriate for their age, while more experienced players can be challenged at a higher level.
Do boys and girls train together?
No. Boys and girls train in separate groups. This gives players their own space to compete, learn and receive meaningful coaching attention.
What ages does LIT train?
LIT works with players from ages 5–18.
Where does LIT train?
District 7, Ho Chi Minh City. Contact Coach for current group details.
How much is training?
Ages 5–11: 350,000 VND per 60-minute session. Ages 12–18: 425,000 VND per 90-minute session.
Can I try a session first?
Yes. A trial session is 400,000 VND. Book a trial today.
