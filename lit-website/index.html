COMMON QUESTIONS FROM
PARENTS AND ATHLETES.

Does my child need prior experience?
No. Athletes join at every level — from their very first session to those already competing at school level. What matters is readiness to learn and respond to coaching.
Who leads the sessions?
Every session is coach-led. Coach Zinger leads the program and remains actively involved in every athlete's development, supported by assistant coaches who hold the same standard.
How large are the groups?
Group sizes are intentionally limited by age group: U7 (8:1) · U9 (10:1) · U11/U13 (10:1) · U16 (12:1). Every athlete receives individual attention and feedback every session.
What is included in the package fee?
All coach-led sessions, one custom LIT Elite jersey, all equipment and facility costs. One price. No hidden fees.
What happens at a trial session?
Your athlete trains in their age group for one full session. You see the coaching style, the environment and how your athlete responds. After the session, we'll help identify the right program fit.
Where is LIT Elite located?
74 Nguyễn Thị Thập, District 7, Ho Chi Minh City. Fully air-conditioned indoor facility.
What are the training schedules?
Weekdays: 6:30–8:00PM · Weekends:12:00–4:00PM
