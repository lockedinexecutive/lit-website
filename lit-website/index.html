WHO ARE WE

THE LIT ELITE DIFFERENCE

WE BUILD ATHLETES FOR LIFE

LIT Elite Basketball is an international youth basketball academy based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, built to help U7, U9, U11, and U15 athletes develop real skill, confidence, discipline, leadership, and resilience through purposeful, high-standard coaching.

Nothing is random. Every drill has a purpose. Every rep is designed to transfer to the game.

Through limited-enrollment training led by an experienced international coach, LIT Elite gives each athlete focused attention, meaningful development, and an environment where they are challenged, supported, and known. More than an academy, LIT Elite is a community committed to developing skilled, coachable, and confident young athletes on and off the court.

WE DON’T BELIEVE IN RANDOM REPS. WE BELIEVE IN PURPOSEFUL DEVELOPMENT.

TRAINING ENVIRONMENT

FACILITIES BUILT FOR DEVELOPMENT

A safe, professional environment is the foundation of real progress. We invest in the details that protect and develop your child.

Joint-Safe Hardwood

High-quality solid wood surface, unlike spongy floors that harm joints long-term. Our court supports healthy muscle development and protects young athletes' knees & ankles.

Air-Conditioned Court

Fully indoor and climate-controlled. Your child trains in a cool, comfortable environment year-round, ensuring maximum focus and safety regardless of the weather outside.

Wilson Basketballs

We use only professional-grade Wilson balls. Proper equipment from day one builds correct grip, shooting technique, and a true feel for the game.
